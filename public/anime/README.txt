Drop anime clips in this folder (or in subfolders to organize by show, e.g.
chainsaw-man/, one-punch-man/, mob-psycho/). They are discovered automatically
and cycled at random on the mirror — no config needed.

Supported: .gif .png .apng .webp .jpg .webm .mp4 .mov

For the mirror look, prefer bright art on a BLACK or transparent background so
it floats in the glass. Transparent .webm or .apng/.png look best; tall/wide
clips are scaled to fit.

Files you drop here are git-ignored (they can be large / not yours to commit).
Tune the rotation speed in components/Anime.js (ROTATE_MS).
